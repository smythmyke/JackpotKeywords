What this report intentionally leaves out

We don't estimate profit margin, units sold, supplier cost, or marketplace saturation. Honest versions of those numbers would require scraping marketplaces (Amazon/Etsy) or reselling paid wholesale data (1688/Alibaba) — which we won't do. Everything above is built from Google first-party data, open datasets, and our own scoring, so you can trust the figures aren't guesses.

All figures above come from Google first-party data (Keyword Planner, Google Trends), Gemini clustering, and free search-suggest — no scraping, no paid third-party data. "Money signals" reflect advertising competition & commercial value, not SEO ranking difficulty. Brand lists are discovery only and do not imply market share.
