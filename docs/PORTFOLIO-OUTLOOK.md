## Scenario definitions

- **LOW** — Bottom-quartile execution. Audience doesn't show up at scale. Distribution channels convert poorly. No marketplace breakouts. Maintenance burden eats expansion time.
- **MEDIUM** — Central case. Each project hits its individual base-case outcome. LinkedIn creator deals convert as expected for GovToolsPro. Etsy positioning works on JackpotKeywords. Canva app launches and earns modestly.
- **HIGH** — Top-quartile. Multiple bets land. Either GovToolsPro hits a Deltek/Unanet partnership early, *or* JackpotKeywords' AEO MCP gets first-mover listicle pickup, *or* MarkItUp's Canva listing performs above expectations. (Assumes 1–2 of these happen, not all three — solo bandwidth caps simultaneous wins.)

## Bottom line

- **Realistic 24-month outcome: ~$650k combined ARR** (MEDIUM band, ~30–35% probability).
- **Plausible HIGH-band outcome: $1.4M–$2.1M combined ARR** (15–20% probability), requires hiring or concentrated focus.
