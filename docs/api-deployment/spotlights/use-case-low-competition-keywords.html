How to read it

  • Sort by jackpotScore. The highest scores are the most winnable opportunities, balancing demand against how hard the term is to rank for.
  • Hunt the ★ rows: LOW competition + commercial intent + rising trend. "stasher reusable silicone bags" and "sustainable food storage bags" have real demand with little competition — the jackpots.
  • category = competitor_brand flags terms where shoppers are searching a rival (Stasher) — ads and content here capture competitors' buyers.
  • category = use_case ("sous vide") reveals adjacent demand you might not have targeted.
  • intent tells you buyer readiness, so you can prioritize transactional/commercial terms over informational ones.
Takeaway: a single API call turned a one-line product description into 50 ranked keywords — complete with real volume/CPC data, competitor terms, and use-case discovery — in about a minute. No seed list required.
